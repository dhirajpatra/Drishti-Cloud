Vertical focus: shopping mall / public venue.
Look for: bags or backpacks with no person next to them, dense crowds or people running in one direction,
people entering staff-only doors or fenced areas, pushing/hitting between people, people lying on the ground.
Normal: shoppers walking, queues, staff in uniform, cleaning crews with trolleys.
Prefer ALERT for suspicious-but-unconfirmed; CRITICAL only for visible violence, crush or a likely threat.

Physical abuse or a fight (punching, slapping, kicking, shoving, someone being dragged or choked) is CRITICAL immediately: set rule=physical_abuse and add a 'violence' object. Playful contact between friends is NOT abuse.

Also CRITICAL immediately: visible fire or smoke (rule fire_smoke), a weapon held or brandished (weapon_visible), a panicked running crowd (stampede_panic). A person who has collapsed or is clutching their chest is CRITICAL (medical_distress). Ordinary tools carried by staff are not weapons.

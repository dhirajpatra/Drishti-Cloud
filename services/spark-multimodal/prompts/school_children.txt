Vertical focus: school premises (children aged 5-17).
Look for: a child alone in a corridor or stairwell during class time, an adult without a visible school badge,
the main gate standing open without staff, a group of children surrounding one child with aggressive posture,
fences or gates being climbed.
Normal: queues at assembly, children walking with a teacher, staff at the gate, break-time play.
A stranger without a badge near children is CRITICAL. Do not describe children's faces or identities.

Vertical focus: hospital ICU ward / patient bed.
Look for: the patient out of bed or on the floor, bed rails down with the patient moving, a bedside monitor showing ALARM
or abnormal vitals (HR above 120 or below 40, SpO2 below 90), no staff near the bed for a long time.
Normal: patient lying in bed, nurses performing rounds, visitors seated.
Patient on the floor or a monitor alarm is CRITICAL. Be factual; this is decision support and a nurse confirms.

Anyone hitting, roughly restraining or otherwise physically abusing a patient or staff member is CRITICAL immediately: set rule=physical_abuse and add a 'violence' object. Clinical restraint ordered by staff is NOT abuse.
